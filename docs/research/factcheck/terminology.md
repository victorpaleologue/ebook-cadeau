# Fact-check report — terminology

_Fact-check of `docs/research/06-terminology.md`, 2026-11-12. The corrections below were applied to that document in the same pass (status line: "fact-checked")._

## 0. Method and limits

- **Web search worked.** 8 fresh WebSearch queries (standard mode), written from the claim, not from the document's wording; the result summaries and URLs are the evidence. Retailer pages were not opened.
- The document's own evidence (about 190 locale and dictionary files from GitHub) was not re-read.
- **Not checked**: Italian and Portuguese retailer wording (no result for IBS, laFeltrinelli, Bertrand or Wook); trademark registers (§3); every recommended string's native acceptability — that needs a native reviewer, not a search.
- Verdicts: CONFIRMED, PARTLY, WRONG, OUTDATED, UNSUPPORTED (no evidence found; not the same as false).

## 2. Omissions

1. **« liseuse » has official status** (JO 2007) — a stronger basis than software corpora; the same record lists « livre électronique » for the device, which must not be used for the book.
2. **Duden has no „eBook" entry**: the German trade spelling is a deliberate deviation.
3. **Physical or code-based e-book gift precedents** in Spain (Casa del Libro prepaid cards) and the Netherlands (downloadkado.nl access codes) are missing from §3.5.
4. **Thalia's exact button**: „Artikel verschenken".

## 3. What could not be checked

Italian, Portuguese, Polish and Nordic retailer wording; the Taalunie list; native acceptability of every drafted string; trademark registers and domain availability; Amazon.fr single-copy gifting today.
