# European web-to-print printers — production, prices, ESG (research notes)

Topic: online printers ordered by file upload, for the eBook Cadeau jacket card (front cover + 10 mm spine + back, QR code inside).
Research date: 2026-11-12. Market: France first, then EU.

## 0. Method and hard limitations (read first)

- Only **13 web searches completed**. The session's shared WebSearch budget (200 calls per turn across all agents) ran out; 4 further queries (presentation folders with spine, dust jackets, small packaging, Onlineprinters folders) were refused. The target of 25+ searches was **not** reached.
- WebFetch is blocked by the sandbox network allowlist (tested: `www.printenbind.nl`, `comparatif-imprimerie.fr`, `www.onlineprinters.fr` all fail DNS). No printer configurator could be opened, so **no price below was read from a live configurator**; prices come from search-result digests of product pages and from third-party comparison pages.
- Confidence tags: **[V2]** = seen in ≥2 independent sources this session, **[V1]** = one source this session, **[M]** = from memory (knowledge cutoff mid-2026), not verified this session. A large share of the eco/social printer section is [M] and must be re-checked before it is used in a decision.
- Prices are HT (excl. VAT) unless stated. "Read 2026-11-12" means the figure appeared in a search digest on that date; the underlying page date is unknown unless stated.

## 1. Key findings

1. **Ownership is concentrated.** Cimpress (Vistaprint's parent) now owns most of the names on the candidate list: Vistaprint, Pixartprinting, Exaprint, Easyflyer, WIRmachenDRUCK, and, since **2 July 2026, SAXOPRINT and viaprinto** bought from CEWE ([Nasdaq, 2026-05-11](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/cimpress-agrees-purchase-saxoprint-and-viaprinto-cewe-2026-05-11), [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/cimpress-completes-acquisition-of-saxoprint-and-viaprinto), [CEWE via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cewe-completes-sale-commercial-online-213408958.html)) [V2]. "Saxoprint (DE, Cewe)" in the brief is therefore out of date. Independent large players: Onlineprinters, Flyeralarm, Unitedprint/print24 [M], Helloprint [M].
2. **Mass web-to-print does not sell single copies.** Minimums found: Pixartprinting invitations from 10 copies, Helloprint greeting cards from 10, Rapid-Flyer double cards from 25, Realisaprint correspondence cards from 50. Single personalised copies need a print-on-demand type service (Print&Bind, Print API, Printful) or a walk-in copy shop.
3. **Unit cost anchor (folded A6-class card, 300–350 g, 4/4):** roughly €0.36–0.59 HT per unit at 100 copies delivered in France; €2–3 per unit for a single POD card before shipping. Evidence in §4.
4. **A 10 mm spine is not a standard configurator option** for folded cards (single central crease is the norm). The closest catalogue matches are Exaprint's custom-fold double card and "2 ou 3 volets" cards, presentation folders with capacity, or a softcover notebook. The folder/dust-jacket/packaging searches could not be run.
5. **French production among the big names:** Exaprint (Mauguio, near Montpellier) is the only large web-to-print brand with confirmed in-house production in France [V2].
6. **"Climate-neutral printing" claims are now legally fragile** under the EU Empowering Consumers directive (EmpCo) per [ClimatePartner](https://www.climatepartner.com/de/klimaneutral-drucken) [V1]; treat offset-based claims as weak ESG evidence and prefer audited management systems (EMAS, ISO 14001/50001) and paper labels (Blauer Engel, FSC/PEFC).

## 5. ESG evidence

### 5.1 What the labels mean (context, [M] unless cited)

- **Imprim'Vert** (FR): site-level label on hazardous-waste handling, safe storage, no CMR-classified products, energy monitoring. A baseline, not a carbon or paper label.
- **FSC / PEFC**: chain of custody for fibre; says nothing about the plant.
- **Blauer Engel** (DE-UZ 195 for printed products) and **EU Ecolabel**: product-level, recycled fibre and chemistry criteria.
- **EMAS** and **ISO 14001 / ISO 50001**: audited environmental and energy management systems; EMAS requires a public, verified environmental statement.
- **"Klimaneutral" / climate-neutral printing**: offset purchase per order. ClimatePartner itself warns that advertising "klimaneutral drucken" without disclosed reductions now risks warnings and injunctions under EmpCo ([ClimatePartner](https://www.climatepartner.com/de/klimaneutral-drucken)) [V1]. The directive's application date (27 September 2026) is [M].
- Social: **SCOP** (worker cooperative), **ESAT / entreprise adaptée** (sheltered and adapted employment), **Print'Ethic** (UNIIC CSR label), Gemeinwohl-Ökonomie balance sheets in DE/AT.

### 5.4 Tentative ESG ranking for French deliveries

This ranking is a hypothesis built on thin evidence, not a finding.

1. **Best environmental and social case, if confirmed:** an EMAS or Blauer Engel printer (Lokay, dieUmweltDruckerei, Oeding) for German/EU deliveries, or a French SCOP or entreprise adaptée (La Contemporaine, Média Graphic, an UNEA-listed shop) for French deliveries. Social value is highest with SCOP/EA/ESAT. All [M].
2. **Best mainstream option for France:** Exaprint — confirmed French plant and free single-point delivery in France — provided its labels check out. Caveat: Cimpress group, trade-oriented.
3. **Best documented mainstream programmes:** Onlineprinters (ESG report, Blue Angel/FSC/PEFC papers) and SAXOPRINT (ISO 14001 + 50001), both shipping from Germany.
4. **Unknown or unpredictable production location:** WIRmachenDRUCK and Helloprint (partner networks), Vistaprint (no French production), Printoclock (in-house share unknown).
5. **Deprioritise for EU customers:** UK production (MOO, Solopress, Mixam UK) — customs border and longer transport.

## 6. Implications for eBook Cadeau

- **Two fulfilment tiers are needed.** Batches of one title (author or publisher, 25–1000 copies) fit mass web-to-print at €0.4–2 per card. Single personalised gift cards do not: use print-at-home, a walk-in copy shop, or a POD API (Print&Bind order API, Print API, Printful) at ≈ €6 landed.
- **Design the PDF for the common denominator:** one sheet, creased, delivered flat. Offer two templates: a single-crease folded card (accepted everywhere) and a double-crease jacket with a 10 mm spine (needs a printer that accepts custom crease positions — unconfirmed anywhere).
- **The printer sees the redemption code.** Any uploaded PDF containing the QR code exposes a bearer secret to the printer's staff and systems, wherever hosted. Codes in professionally printed batches should stay inactive until the seller activates them [design suggestion, not a researched fact].
- **Do not relay "climate-neutral" claims** from a printer in the product's own marketing; cite audited labels instead.
